# Moddog name and logo

The Moddog **code** is licensed under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **name "Moddog"**, the dog logo and the "Let the dog sniff it first" tagline are not covered by that license. They belong to Matteo Rannou Le Texier ([@matteorlt](https://github.com/matteorlt)), the creator of the project.

This protects users: when people install something called Moddog, they should get the real, official project, and not a lookalike that could itself be malicious.

## ✅ You can, without asking

- Fork the project, modify it and share your version **under a different name and logo**
- Say your project is "based on Moddog" or "a fork of Moddog"
- Write about Moddog, make videos, tutorials and reviews, and use the name and logo to refer to the official project
- Use the name in pull requests and contributions to this repository

## ❌ Please don't

- Publish an extension, app or website named "Moddog" (or a confusingly similar name) that isn't the official project
- Use the logo for your own fork or product
- Suggest that your version is official or endorsed when it isn't

Questions? Open an issue.
